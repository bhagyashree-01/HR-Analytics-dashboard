# HR-Analytics-dashboard
The HR Analytics Dashboard helps HR teams and management monitor, analyze, and improve workforce performance. It consolidates employee-related data such as headcount, attendance, performance, attrition, and engagement into interactive charts and KPIs. This dashboard supports data-driven HR decisions by presenting real-time and historical workforce trends in an easy-to-understand format.

# Objectives of HR Analytics Dashboard 
-Monitor overall employee headcount and workforce structure
-Analyze employee performance and productivity trends
-Track attrition, retention, and turnover rates
-Identify attendance, absenteeism, and overtime patterns
-Evaluate employee engagement and satisfaction levels
-Support data-driven HR decision-making
-Improve workforce planning and talent management

# Key Insights from HR Analytics Dashboard

1.Overall Workforce Insights
-The organization has 1470 total employees
-Out of these, 1233 are currently active
-The average employee age is 37 years, indicating a mid-level experienced workforce

2.Attrition Overview
-Total employees who left the organization: 237
-Overall attrition rate is 16%, which is moderately high
-Attrition is a key concern for workforce stability

3.Department-Wise Attrition Insights
-Sales department has the highest attrition, showing retention challenges
-R&D department also contributes significantly to attrition
-HR department has the lowest attrition, indicating better employee stability

4.Age Group & Gender Distribution
-Most employees fall in the 25–34 and 35–44 age groups
-Workforce size decreases significantly for Under 25 and Over 55 age groups
-Male employees are more dominant than female employees across most age bands

5.Attrition by Age Group & Gender
-The 25–34 age group shows the highest attrition, indicating early-career job switching
-Attrition is higher among male employees in most age categories
-Employees aged 45–54 show lower attrition, suggesting higher job stability

6.Education Field-Wise Attrition
-Employees from Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds have higher attrition
-Human Resources and Other education fields show minimal attrition
-Certain educational backgrounds may require targeted engagement strategies

7.Job Satisfaction Insights
-Job satisfaction levels vary significantly across different job roles
-Sales Executives and Research Scientists have the largest workforce
-Some roles show higher counts in low satisfaction ratings (1 & 2), indicating potential disengagement risks

8.Key Business Insights
-Young professionals and sales-focused roles are at higher attrition risk
-Retention strategies should focus on career growth, compensation, and engagement
-Improving job satisfaction can help reduce attrition and improve employee retention

Overall Insight
This dashboard helps HR teams identify high-risk attrition areas, understand workforce demographics, and take data-driven actions to improve employee satisfaction, retention, and organizational performance.
